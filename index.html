人生支線 Privacy Policy · 私隱政策

Last updated / 最後更新: 2026-07

中文

人生支線唔會收集你嘅任何個人資料。

  • 相片： 你為任務加嘅證明相，只會儲存喺你部裝置入面，永遠唔會上傳到任何伺服器。相片透過系統相片選擇器存取，App冇你成個相簿嘅讀取權限。
  • 遊戲進度： 你嘅等級、任務紀錄同印章全部儲存喺裝置本機。冇帳戶、冇雲端同步。
  • 網絡： 本版本完全離線運作，唔會發出任何網絡請求。
  • 追蹤： 冇分析工具、冇廣告、冇第三方SDK。
  • 通知： 如你允許，App會發出每週一次嘅本地提醒。呢啲通知由你部機自己排程，唔涉及任何伺服器。

    • 刪除App即代表刪除所有資料。

    English

    人生支線 (Life Side Quest) collects no personal data.

  • Photos: proof photos are stored on your device only and are never

    • uploaded anywhere. They are accessed through the system photo picker;

    the app has no access to your photo library.

  • Game progress: your level, quest history and stamps are stored locally

    • on-device. There are no accounts and no cloud sync.

  • Network: this version works fully offline and makes no network requests.
  • Tracking: no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
  • Notifications: with your permission, the app schedules one weekly local

    • reminder on your device. No server is involved.

    Deleting the app deletes all data.
