人生支線 Privacy Policy · 私隱政策

Last updated / 最後更新: 2026-09

中文

我哋收集得越少越好。具體如下：

  • 相片：你為任務加嘅證明相，只會儲存喺你部裝置入面，永遠唔會上傳到任何伺服器。相片透過系統相片選擇器或相機存取，App冇你成個相簿嘅讀取權限。
  • 遊戲進度：你嘅等級、任務紀錄同印章儲存喺裝置本機，冇雲端同步。
  • 冒險小隊（自選功能）：如果你選擇開隊或者入隊，App會為你建立一個匿名帳戶（冇姓名、電郵或電話），並將你自己揀嘅暱稱、職業、等級、小隊名，同埋你完成任務嘅標題，經 Google Firebase（伺服器位於亞洲）同你嘅隊友同步。呢啲資料只有你小隊嘅成員睇到。離開小隊或刪除帳戶會刪除相關資料。唔用小隊功能，App唔會建立任何帳戶。
  • 使用統計：App使用 Google Analytics for Firebase 收集匿名嘅使用數據（例如「今日有幾多次抽卡」），用嚟改善產品。呢啲數據唔會連結到你嘅身份，冇廣告，冇跨App追蹤，唔使用IDFA。
  • 通知：如你允許，App會發出每週一次嘅本地提醒，由你部機自己排程。
  • 刪除：App內「我」→「刪除帳戶同所有資料」會刪除裝置上同伺服器上嘅一切。

English

We collect as little as possible. Specifically:

  • Photos: proof photos are stored on your device only and are never uploaded. They are accessed through the system photo picker or camera; the app cannot read your photo library.
  • Game progress: your level, quest history and stamps are stored locally on-device, with no cloud sync.
  • Adventure parties (optional): if you choose to create or join a party, the app creates an anonymous account (no name, email or phone) and syncs your chosen nickname, class, level, party name and the titles of quests you complete with your party members via Google Firebase (servers in Asia). Only your party members can see this. Leaving the party or deleting your account removes this data. If you never use parties, no account is created.
  • Usage statistics: the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage data (e.g. how many draws happened today) to improve the product. This data is not linked to your identity; there are no ads, no cross-app tracking, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.
  • Notifications: with your permission, the app schedules one weekly local reminder on your device.
  • Deletion: in-app, Profile → "Delete account and all data" removes everything on-device and on the server.
